FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What is an AI voice receptionist?

An AI voice receptionist is software that answers your business phone 24/7, talks to callers in natural speech, answers common questions and books appointments directly into your calendar. Advaya AI's receptionists respond in under 300ms and hand off to a human when needed.

Can an AI receptionist book appointments for a dental clinic?

Yes. Advaya AI's dental front-desk agent answers patient calls, triages toothache emergencies, verifies basic insurance details and books open chair slots directly into Dentrix or Google Calendar, including after hours. See our dental automation service →

How does a WhatsApp booking bot help a local business?

A WhatsApp booking bot replies to new leads within seconds, asks qualifying questions, shares service details and books a consultation. Fast replies stop ad leads going cold and raise conversion rates.

Will the AI double-book appointments or quote wrong prices?

No. Advaya AI uses deterministic rules and state guardrails, so availability and pricing come from your real calendar and price list rather than being generated by the AI model.

Which tools does Advaya AI integrate with?

Advaya AI integrates with Google Calendar, Dentrix, HighLevel, ServiceTitan, Jobber, the Meta WhatsApp Business API, payment links and the Google Business Profile API, plus custom software through APIs.

How much revenue do missed calls cost a local business?

A business with 120 monthly inquiries, a 20% missed-call rate and a $450 average job value can recover about $32,400 per year by answering every call instantly, assuming a conservative 25% conversion rate on recovered leads. Try the calculator →
